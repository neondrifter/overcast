Welcome to Overcast!

Do you ever find yourself scrolling your Bluesky feed and coming across content you didn't want to see?

Sure, Bluesky has content filtration tools but, if the content isn't tagged properly, those tools fail to do the job.

And more than that, what if you're trying to avoid more than adult content? What if you're trying to avoid spoilers? Or maybe you just want a less media heavy experience altogether?

Well, let's throw some clouds into that Bluesky and experience Overcast!

<img width="663" height="969" alt="overcast example feed" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9a67ac56-a783-4751-be82-1301c7857f9f" />

Overcast is a fork of the Bluesky app that allows you to toggle media content with easy tap-and-reveal features.

You can either enable Overcast within the settings for your whole feed or have it turned off and the app will work normally.

With the setting on however, all media embedded posts will have a cloud overlay on them, and will only reveal the contents of the post with a simple tap. Didn't like what it revealed? Easily put the clouds back on with a tap on the pill button at the top right of the media.
